Hazard rules

Visual hole designer

Choose a hole and a placement tool. Left-click repeatedly to add bunkers, ponds, trees, tree groves, hills or hollows. Drag the flag to reposition the green and the gold marker to reposition the dogleg turn. Add fairway waypoints, then drag their blue handles to shape winding fairways. Adjust green size with the slider. Right-click or press Esc to stop placing and return to Select / move. Select an object to drag, resize or delete it. Use Erase objects to click-delete, or pick an item from Object list when objects overlap. Undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Y, and delete selected objects with Delete or Backspace. Your edits affect gameplay.

Selected object

Select a bunker, pond or tree on the plan.

Drag the green flag to change the dogleg, or drag the gold bend marker to change where the fairway turns. Use grove brush for clusters of trees, or hill/hollow brushes to sculpt terrain. The green and fairway update live. Sculpting is local and the tee is protected from terrain brushes; tee elevation is changed only with the Tee elevation field. Changes are saved with your course.

Current round

🏆 Solo tournament championship

Create a course rotation, play each nine-hole round, and track your performance across tournaments. Courses are played in the order selected. Your previous tournaments remain available for comparison.

Past tournaments & improvement

Scores are compared relative to par across different courses. This is a solo stroke-play challenge, not an official handicap-rated competition. Each finished round is also saved in your player history. Export clubhouse JSON to back up tournament history.
